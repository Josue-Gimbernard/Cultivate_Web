# Owner Answer Log

This is the durable record for owner answers. Do not treat an answer as approved public copy until it is captured here and reflected in the approval matrix.

## Status Key

- Pending: no owner answer yet.
- Approved: owner confirmed the answer and it can move to approval review.
- Revise: owner answered, but wording or recommendation needs another pass.
- Hold: keep private; do not use in public draft copy.
- Validate: collect evidence or operational details before public use.
