Draft stance Home away from home.

This page should feel exciting to come to, relieving to have close by, and independent but together.

For families

Not a school. Not a tutoring center. Not a daycare.

Cultivate is something bigger and more human: a living, working community where teens build real-world skills and self-awareness, kids grow, families connect, and everyone has a place.

KidsProjects, life skills, confidence, and belonging.
TeensResponsibility, mentorship, creativity, and community.
ParentsWorkspace, connection, contribution, and breathing room.

The first version

Start with interest, then build what families actually need.

The first public move should invite families to join the interest list. It should not imply enrollment, payment, a fixed location, or a launch date.

The Summit

The teen community space for ages 13-18, centered on real-life skills, belonging, and self-awareness.

The Stepping Stones

Ages 6-12, included if family demand and qualified staff support it.

The Grove

A parent co-working lounge with self-serve coffee station and comfortable seating.

The Village Wall

A founding community display with build-out photos, staff, ribbon cutting, and founding families.

Still in validation

The bigger vision stays visible, but bounded.

The Sprout Space, Harvest Cafe, Foxtail, paid teen work, apprenticeships, Commons rentals, specific pricing, and full outdoor campus should remain private or future-facing until validated.
