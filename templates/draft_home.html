For families

A place for the whole homeschool rhythm.

Cultivate is being shaped as a community-centered place for teens, middle kids, and parents: a place to practice useful skills, make real contributions, work near one another, and build a village with intention.

KidsProjects, life skills, confidence, and belonging.
TeensResponsibility, mentorship, creativity, and community.
ParentsWorkspace, connection, contribution, and breathing room.

The first version

A founding cohort before the full campus.

The first public story should point to a limited founding cohort, not the whole future campus. The goal is to test rhythm, trust, demand, and operational fit with care.

The Summit

A teen community rhythm centered on real-life skills, belonging, and meaningful contribution.

The Stepping Stones

Hands-on project blocks for middle kids, designed to build confidence and curiosity.

The Grove

A parent landing place for work, connection, and community contribution during cohort time.

The Commons

A flexible workshop and gathering space for the first cohort's shared rhythm.

Still in validation

The bigger vision stays visible, but bounded.

The Sprout Space, Harvest Cafe, Foxtail idea, paid teen roles, rentals, pricing, dates, and full outdoor campus should remain future vision or internal planning until approved.
