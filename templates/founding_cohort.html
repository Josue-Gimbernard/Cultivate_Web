Recommended first launch

Founding Cohort Term

A small, careful first term that proves the culture and rhythm before Cultivate promises the full campus.

Plain version

Start with the families who want to help build the village.

The first version should test teen belonging, middle-kid projects, parent community, and the five-week rhythm. It should not depend on licensed childcare, public cafe operations, paid teen jobs, or a full outdoor campus.

Working shape

  • 8-12 families
  • One 5-week term
  • 2-3 days per week
  • Teens and middle kids as the anchor
  • Parents invited into workspace and contribution rhythm

Include

Safe to shape now

  • The Summit Lite
  • The Stepping Stones Lite
  • Parent workspace/community area
  • Workshop and gathering room
  • Village Wall and project showcase
  • Family skills inventory

Defer

Validate before promising

  • Licensed childcare
  • Public cafe
  • Foxtail partnership
  • Paid teen employment
  • Public rentals
  • Full outdoor play buildout

Five-week rhythm

Build the Village.

  1. 1Belonging

    Orient families, gather needs, and establish the culture.

  2. 2Food and care

    Explore hospitality and communal rhythm without operating as a cafe.

  3. 3Making and repair

    Test hands-on life skills and project ownership.

  4. 4Outdoors

    Prototype stewardship, garden thinking, and outdoor rhythm lightly.

  5. 5Open and reflect

    Showcase projects, collect feedback, and shape the next term.
